# Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard

This repository presents a comprehensive analysis of marketing campaign performance, visualized through an interactive dashboard. The dashboard provides insights into campaign success, spending trends, income analysis, purchase channels, complaint trends, and spending distribution by education level.

---

## Dashboard Metrics and Visualizations

### 1. Campaign Success Distribution
- **Top Campaigns**: Campaign 4 (25.04%) and Campaign 5 (24.44%) are the most successful campaigns.
- **Least Successful Campaign**: Campaign 2, accounting for only 4.44% of the total.

### 2. Income & Spending Trends by Quarter and Year
- **Peak Income**: Q1 2013 with $16.5M.
- **Spending Trends**: Steady decrease after Q1 2013, reaching a low of $124K in Q2 2014.

### 3. Spending vs. Income Analysis by Day
- A positive correlation exists between spending and income, with higher spending observed in higher income brackets.

### 4. Purchase Channel Breakdown
- **Top Channel**: Store Purchases dominate with 38.96%.
- **Other Channels**:
  - Web Purchases: 27.48%
  - Catalog Purchases: 17.91%
  - Deals Purchases: 15.64%

### 5. Complaint Trends Over Time
- **Highest Complaints**: Q1 and Q2 of 2013.
- **Decreasing Trend**: Complaints have declined steadily since Q2 2013.

### 6. Spending Distribution by Education Level
- **Graduates** are the largest contributors to spending, particularly in categories such as Fish Products, Fruits, and Wines.
- **Lower Spending Groups**: Basic and 2nd Cycle education levels.

---

## Key Conclusions
- **Campaign Performance**: Campaign 4 has been the most impactful, while Campaign 2 needs further optimization or discontinuation.
- **Spending Analysis**: Income and spending have a clear correlation, but spending trends have declined over recent quarters.
- **Purchase Behavior**: Most purchases occur through physical stores, highlighting the need to focus more on digital and catalog channels.
- **Customer Profile**: Graduates represent the highest spending group, suggesting campaigns targeted towards educated demographics yield better returns.

---

## How to Use
The dashboard visualizations can guide marketing teams to:
1. Optimize campaign strategies.
2. Identify high-value customers and target groups.
3. Address complaints in a timely manner.
4. Reallocate resources to high-performing channels and product categories.
